---
name: Inventory group variants
description: How related inventory items share a group while keeping distinct billable units.
---

Items created as siblings retain the selected item's group number, but remain separate records with their own prices, unit types, barcodes, colors, and quick-sale flags. Invoices should let users choose and bill each unit row at its own price and show the unit label.

**Why:** The user requested adding an item in the same group without sharing its pricing or unit details.

**How to apply:** Keep sibling products as separate inventory items; present individual unit rows as separate invoice choices rather than collapsing their prices.
